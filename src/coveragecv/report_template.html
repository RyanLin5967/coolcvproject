Supervision contract

What the compiler guarantees

  • Missing coverage declarations default to unknown.
  • Observed positives retain their entire matched loss.
  • Unknown classes supply no unjustified negative classification gradient.
  • Contradictions, changed artifacts and split collisions fail validation.

Roboflow integration

What is and isn't established

  • Numerical parity and gradient behavior are executable tests against RF-DETR 1.10.1.
  • The source declarations express policy; the compiler cannot certify a human labeled every object.
  • Partial-label learning is established prior work. This project contributes a coverage-preserving compiler and a tested integration.
  • The custom loss runs in our trainer. Ordinary hosted training does not automatically consume its sidecar.
